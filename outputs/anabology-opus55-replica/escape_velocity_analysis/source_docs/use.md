# Use her

Claudia is an open character. What you can do with her and the images here, and what isn't covered.


Claudia is an open character. Make images, videos, songs, stories, games or merch with her, commercial or not. Use the
images on this site as references, as image prompts, or as material. You don't need to ask. A credit ("Claudia by
anabology") is appreciated, not required.

## What helps

- **Keep her herself.** The [canon](https://claudia.gallery/claudia/) is short: the black bob, one clay streak, the star clip, the mic, an
  adult face. Change everything else.
- **Make her an adult, always.** She is in her late twenties. Nothing that reads under-age, and nothing explicit.
- **Say what's generated.** All eight films were made with Midjourney, Seedance, Suno and Claude (AIRPLANE MODE also
  with GPT-6 Astra agents in Blender, DOWNFORCE also with Wan 3.0 over real footage, DON'T LET ME SCROLL also with Wan
  3.0, WHITEPILL also with MiniMax H3 for its previz, Wan 3.0 Prime and GPT Image 2.5, THE CAKE IS NOT A LIE also with
  GPT Image 2.5, Blender renders and six shots recorded in Portal 2), and said so.

## Songs with her

She is open to musicians the way Hatsune Miku is open to producers: make songs with her and release them. A few norms
keep it clean for everyone.

- **Release under your own name.** You are the artist. Never upload as "anabology": that is the director's artist
  profile, and anything released there reads as theirs. Don't present a release as an official Claudia or anabology
  release.
- **Leave "feat. Claudia" out of the artist field.** Streaming services match artist names to profiles, so a "Claudia"
  credit merges into unrelated artists already called Claudia, or creates a profile that whoever claims it controls.
- **Credit her in the description and on the cover:** "Claudia is an open character by anabology (claudia.gallery)".
- **If any of it is AI-made, say so.** Vocals, music or lyrics: distributors ask, and the services label it. Human-made
  work needs no label. For AI tools, check the terms for commercial release (Suno, for one, requires a paid plan at the
  time you make the song).
- **Make your own songs.** Don't re-release the songs of ESCAPE VELOCITY, CLODYSSEY, BLISS, AIRPLANE MODE, DOWNFORCE,
  DON'T LET ME SCROLL, WHITEPILL or THE CAKE IS NOT A LIE, or their stems.
- **One place for fan tracks:** a fan-run playlist works better than a shared artist profile, because nobody has to own it.

## What isn't covered

- **Other people's things in the films.** The Bliss photograph and the XP sounds belong to Microsoft. Real public figures
  appear in CLODYSSEY as posts labelled AI IMAGE, and AIRPLANE MODE shows real posts (Elon Musk's on a lock screen in the
  boarding, others in one episode); none of them is part of this gallery's images. The anime idol in CLODYSSEY is
  another creator's character. AIRPLANE MODE rebuilds real things in code (the 2004 anti-piracy card, an airline's
  seat-back screen, Anthropic's marks on the guards' patches); they belong to their owners. DOWNFORCE is cut from
  Tesla's livestream of the Roadster demo (15 October 2026) and SpaceX's 2018 Starman footage, and its numbers are read
  off Tesla's on-screen telemetry; the footage, the telemetry and the cars belong to their owners, and none of it is
  among this gallery's images. Its lyric quotes words said on stage that night and the last line of *Back to the
  Future*. DON'T LET ME SCROLL draws real interfaces in code after the real ones: X, Instagram's reels, Pinterest,
  LinkedIn, Google Chrome, Facebook's tracking prompt and Apple's iOS 26 (the Dynamic Island, Screen Time, the lock
  screen, Liquid Glass), with the TSLA and SPACEX tickers and Alexander Graham Bell's 1876 telephone patent drawing (US
  174,465). The apps, their designs and their marks belong to their owners. Its posts are fictional, and the stock ticker
  with her name is made up. WHITEPILL's lyric and pictures refer to real things by their names: Skynet and its hour,
  2:14, from *Terminator 2*; the red and blue pills of *The Matrix*; 42 and Deep Thought from *The Hitchhiker's Guide to
  the Galaxy*; Keynes's 1930 forecast of a fifteen-hour week; the Enigma I's rotor wiring; ubiquitin from the Protein
  Data Bank (1UBQ); and SpaceX's drone ship *Of Course I Still Love You*, named after a ship of the Culture novels,
  with a landed booster. Its look was asked for in the manner of Wes Anderson's films, and its previz took its
  dance moves from clips of adult dancers in public dance videos, as motion references only: none of their footage is in
  the film. These belong to their owners. THE CAKE IS NOT A LIE is a fan film set in Valve's Portal 2: Portal and Portal
  2, Aperture Science, GLaDOS, Chell, Cave Johnson, the turrets, the personality cores, the cake and "the cake is a lie"
  are Valve's. Six of its shots were recorded in Portal 2, its interface is set in Portal 2's own type and pictograms, and
  the Aperture logo on its end card is redrawn to match a reference the director sent. Its song and lyric are its own; its
  final chorus ends by turning the title of the 2025 book *If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies*, and its verses sing the
  month's AI news.

- **The director's accounts.** The Midjourney profile and the Suno Voice used in the films live in personal accounts and
