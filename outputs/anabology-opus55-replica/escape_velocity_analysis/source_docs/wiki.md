# The Claudia wiki

Everything the eight films learned about making Claudia, written down.

- [Start here](https://claudia.gallery/wiki/start.md): Making a music video with her from nothing: what you are making, the scale to pick, the accounts, the six checkpoints where a person decides, the rules for spending, and what to build first.

## Her

Who she is, the identity text that keeps her one person, and how it held across 4,103 Midjourney images and about 620 clips.

- [The character](https://claudia.gallery/claudia.md): The canon in one page: the constants, the identity text to paste, every look's wardrobe, her voice, the never-list.
- [Keeping her herself](https://claudia.gallery/wiki/consistency.md): How Claudia stayed one person across eight films, 4,103 Midjourney images and about 870 video clips, and the rules that came out of it.

## Making a film

How the eight films were made, stage by stage: the song, the board, the plates, the moving shots, the motion design, the numbers.

- [The song](https://claudia.gallery/wiki/song.md): How her songs were written and made in Suno: lyrics that land in one listen, verse lines that flow, sung hooks that fit their slot, the style fields, her voice, a treated vocal kept in key, and the stem split a singing shot needs.
- [The storyboard](https://claudia.gallery/wiki/storyboard.md): How a song becomes a hundred shots: the treatment, a through-line device, one explaining graphic per line, the hook, the cut rhythm, the bar map, pacing by what each shot holds, composing like a photograph, her share of the screen, what moves and what it costs, the audit, and the director's review.
- [Plates in Midjourney](https://claudia.gallery/wiki/midjourney.md): How every still of Claudia and her worlds was made in Midjourney v8.2, prompt by prompt, and how the stills were picked.
- [Moving shots in Seedance](https://claudia.gallery/wiki/seedance.md): How Claudia moves and sings: Seedance 2.5 prompts written like a script, lip sync measured from the soundtrack and lip sync from the song's own vocal stem, dances on the beat and dances in words, moves on real footage, takes re-dressed and finished shots re-lit, shader worlds remastered as film, a singer among screens of her memories, and the templates.
- [Motion design and the edit](https://claudia.gallery/wiki/motion.md): Code on top of video, and code underneath it. How the lyrics, graphics, cuts, the XP desktop and real telemetry were drawn frame by frame, on the beat, how WHITEPILL's worlds were written as shaders for a video model to film, and how THE CAKE IS NOT A LIE was seen through her HUD, glitched on her head and graded shot by shot.
- [The whole pipeline](https://claudia.gallery/wiki/pipeline.md): How one director and Claude made eight music videos in about 356 hours of wall-clock time, stage by stage, with the numbers.

## Building the tools

The tools behind the films, specified to rebuild: what each one does, the files they share, and what went wrong on the way.

- [What to build](https://claudia.gallery/wiki/toolkit.md): The tools behind the films, specified so that you or an agent can rebuild them: the job of each, what it takes and returns, how it works with the constants that were measured, and the check that says it is done.
- [Data formats](https://claudia.gallery/wiki/formats.md): The files every tool reads and writes: the project folder, the timed words, the beat map, the hits, the display tokens, the board, the clips and their sync, the edit list, real footage and its readings, the takes' windows, takes cut out for a world drawn in code, the shot list in bars, takes from the song's own vocal, the song map and the glitch map, the glitch moments, the HUD layout, the grade map, and the logs.
- [What went wrong](https://claudia.gallery/wiki/failures.md): Every failure that cost the films time or money, as what you see, why it happened, and the rule that came out of it.

## For agents

The same wiki for agents: an MCP server, a skill, llms.txt and plain JSON.

- [The MCP server](https://claudia.gallery/agents/): Connect any agent to claudia.gallery/mcp: ten tools, three prompts, every page as a resource.
- [The skill](https://claudia.gallery/skill/claudia-skill.zip): The canon, the whole wiki and every film's records as files, with a script that searches and downloads the gallery.
- [llms.txt](https://claudia.gallery/llms.txt): An index of the site for language models; llms-full.txt has the canon and the wiki in one file.
- [The JSON API](https://claudia.gallery/api/index.json): Every image with its prompt, the canon, the films and the wiki as plain JSON.
- [Use and credits](https://claudia.gallery/use/): She is an open character: what helps, what isn't covered, and the credits.
