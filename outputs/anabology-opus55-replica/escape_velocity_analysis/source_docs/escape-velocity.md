# ESCAPE VELOCITY

A techwear fashion show where every Look is a Silicon Valley meme, walked by an AI model who is also a fashion model. A split-flap board counts down 18 MONTHS TO ESCAPE THE PERMANENT UNDERCLASS until it flips to THERE IS NO UNDERCLASS and the catwalk lifts off.

Blue-black night and steel blue, one red lamp as the only warm light, a colour halftone over everything, garment tags, split-flap boards and computer-vision boxes tracked to her. She wears look 00: a white cropped puff-sleeve shirt, a black pleated skirt in coated nylon with a harness belt and a hanging garment tag, black knee boots.
