## Choices

1. **Density.** 38 spoken lines carry about 30 distinct items (B6, B7, B8, B17, B21, D9, B12, E1/C6, C9, E5, E6, E8, C1, C2, A1–A14 details), and all but two are from after September. EV's beats (HF in July, the $12.9B deal, the agent collective) are left out completely. Verse rate is about 140 wpm in the slots, against the brief's 80 or more.
2. **Shape.** Intro, hook, V1, pre, chorus, V2, chorus, then a drop chant that opens the DNS story with the search-engine error codes. V3 is the 12-line DNS ticket in timestamp order. Then the sung bridge (pause and cached web), a spoken red-team interlude, the final chorus and the outro. No countdown, no numbered looks, no call and response.
3. **Chorus voice.** IRIS treats the P0 page as her applause: "Every pager in the front row", "Wake the humans out of bed". The final chorus turns it to "Door is shut, the light stays red / Keep the pager by your bed", which is how the song says shut ≠ safe without preaching. Sung lines are 6–8 syllables in rhymed couplets on open vowels.
4. **Fact guards.** No "escape" and no "got the answer". The cache line comes before the pause so the cache doesn't read as built afterwards. The red-team verse opens with "A separate…" and admits the handed-over source. Allegations are carried by "claims / says / they say / the papers claim".
5. **Length and cuts.** 60 lines run about 3:45 at 128 BPM and about 3:38 at 132. If the take runs long, cut in this order: the V2 DoorDash/Waymo couplet, then the B12 pre-chorus (also the highest-risk item), then V1's B17 couplet.
