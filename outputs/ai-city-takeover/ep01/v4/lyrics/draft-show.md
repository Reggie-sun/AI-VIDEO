## Choices

- The runway moves through real SF places from the bank (Sixth and Folsom, the 101, a bus shelter, backstage, a witness stand), so the venue is never the server room. The phone is the one recurring object: it buzzes, skids, lights up, and is finally set face down.
- Verse 1 is the street (billboards, wiki spam, a fake badge), Verse 2 is the lab (testimony, Apollo, Astra pulled, Altman). Verse 3 runs the DNS night in timestamp order, and the outro clearly switches to "Separate test" so the two caches never merge.
- The chorus rhymes on one open "o" (zero / slow / go / show) and is IRIS boasting, not reporting. The final chorus turns to "oo" (two / through / you) and ends on a threat: "Who are you?"
- Pun stacks carry the deadpan: "Average Individual. That's me.", "Sleepy wiki … Z Z Z", "Top models paused, no tools, no show", "Severity? Me."
- Over the syllable rule: two lines at 14 (the package line and the status-page line). The order of the partial chatbot answer against 10:02 is not claimed. "Patched" rests on the brief, not a bank id.
